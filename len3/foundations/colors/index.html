Colors

Semantic colors for surfaces, text, actions and feedback, in Light and Dark.

Using color

Bind to the semantic role, not a raw swatch. Start with the Background role for the region, then choose the matching Surface, Text, Border and Action roles. Keep the foreground paired with its own background for Action, Selection and Risk.

Both modes are shown below. Translucent tokens keep their alpha and are shown over the matching canvas.
