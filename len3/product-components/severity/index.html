Severity

Identify the risk level of a report finding.

Preview

Live preview
Medium
Figma specimen Web preview only

Usage

Use High, Medium or Low alongside the finding title. Keep the risk in text so the badge is readable without color. Use Badge for statuses unrelated to risk.

Uses the Risk fill and foreground pair. The badge has 6px horizontal padding, 2px vertical padding, 6px radius and no visible border.
